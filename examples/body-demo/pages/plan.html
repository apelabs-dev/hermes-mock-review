Acme Notes 2.0 launch plan

Four parts. Mark each one: Approve it, ask for a Change, or Skip it. Add a note where it helps.

What we say

Acme Notes 2.0 is for people who keep notes in three places and trust none of them. Everything we publish comes back to three points.

  • One inbox for every note. Type it, paste it or share it in; it lands in the same place.
  • Search that finds a phrase, not just a title, even inside long notes.
  • Works without a signal. Edits are saved on the device first and sync when you are back online.

Launch week

Release on Monday morning, then one thing a day so the team can answer questions the day they come in.

Where we announce it

Three places, each with a job. Nothing is posted anywhere else.

  • EmailThe one message existing users get. Short, with a link to the walkthrough.
  • Site and blogRelease notes with a short screen recording of each of the three points.
  • Community forumA pinned thread where Support answers within a working day.
